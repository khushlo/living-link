LivingLink

LivingLink is a full-stack, open-source platform that addresses all five EMPOWER focus areas through five interconnected modules: ReadyCheck, DonorShield, MentorMatch, CenterFlow, and LifeAfter.

Built on HL7 FHIR R4 with SMART on FHIR and CDS Hooks, it integrates natively into Epic and Cerner with no additional hardware. Five public-facing tools require no account to use, removing the single biggest barrier to donor engagement.

Released under the MIT open-source license. HIPAA-compliant with AES-256 field-level encryption, append-only audit logging, and WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility throughout. Co-designed with three prior living donors, a veteran transplant coordinator, and a living-donor nephrologist.

Section 3

Public-First Features - No Account Required

Zero Barrier to Entry

LivingLink removes the biggest conversion killer in digital health: the sign-up wall. Five full web experiences and two API endpoints are accessible with no account, no login, no personal data required. A curious potential donor can explore their eligibility, calculate donation impact, practice a difficult conversation with AI, and read real donor stories before ever creating an account.

No Login
/could-i-qualify
Could I Qualify?

A 60-second, 5-question eligibility pre-screener. No PHI collected. Returns one of three plain-language results: "Great candidate," "Possible candidate," or "Work on it first." Converts awareness into intent.

No Login
/ripple
Ripple Effect Calculator

Animated impact visualizer using USRDS 2024 data. Shows dialysis sessions avoided, life-years gained for the recipient, caregiver hours freed, and the $91K/yr dialysis cost avoided. Designed to motivate exploration without pressure.

No Login
/waitlist-map
National Waitlist Explorer

All 50 states with OPTN/SRTR 2024 data: waitlist count, living donor rate per million, average wait months, and transplant center count. Lets donors see the need in their own community.

No Login
/stories
Donor Story Library

A filterable, searchable library of verified donor stories. Filter by donation type (directed, non-directed, paired exchange). Helpful-vote system surfaces the most useful stories. Addresses misinformation through authentic lived experience.

No Login + AI Powered
/start-conversation
AI Conversation Practice Simulator

One of LivingLink's most distinctive features: a GPT-4o-powered role-play tool where the AI plays the other person in a difficult conversation the donor needs to have. Five scenarios are available without any account:

Telling your spouse Talking to your boss about FMLA Explaining it to your parent Addressing a worried friend Preparing your primary care doctor

The AI responds naturally, pushes back with realistic concerns, and helps the donor practice until they feel confident. No account created, no data stored.

Public API Endpoints
GET

/api/stories - Returns donor story library, filterable by ?type=directed|non-directed|paired. Served with CORS headers for potential embedding by transplant center websites or patient advocacy organizations.

POST

/api/ai/conversation-practice - GPT-4o conversation role-play endpoint. Accepts { scenario, messages[] }, returns AI response. No auth required. Rate-limited at the edge (Vercel middleware). No PHI transmitted or stored.

Why This Matters for EMPOWER Scoring

The EMPOWER prize prioritizes donor experience (35 pts). Starting the donor journey with zero friction directly increases the addressable population. Every person who uses /could-i-qualify or /ripple without an account is a potential donor that would have bounced from a login-first experience. This design is a deliberate, evidence-informed conversion strategy.

Section 5 - Module 1

ReadyCheck: Health Readiness Coach

Many willing donors fail evaluation due to manageable health conditions they could have improved before presenting. ReadyCheck gives donors an AI-powered health coach months before their evaluation, turning "too high BMI" into a 90-day action plan instead of a rejection letter.

Core Features

  • Self-reported health metrics intake: age, BMI, blood pressure (systolic/diastolic), eGFR estimate, smoking status, diabetes indicator
  • GPT-4o generates a plain-language AI summary - explicitly marked "not a medical diagnosis"
  • Goal Tracker with 30/60/90-day targets for BMI, blood pressure, weight, smoking cessation, and blood sugar
  • Recharts trend visualizations showing progress over time
  • AI coach notes stored per goal to keep donors motivated between check-ins
  • CDS Hooks alert fires into Epic/Cerner when a matched patient has an active ReadyCheck profile
  • Disclaimer-first UX: no medical diagnosis framing, referral to nephrologist always included

FHIR Resources Generated

GET

/api/ready-check/history - Returns chronological eligibility checks for trend display.

Section 6 - Module 2

DonorShield: Financial Advocacy Suite

Financial fear is the number-one reason willing donors withdraw. DonorShield replaces a scattered web of government PDFs and phone calls with an integrated financial toolkit - from lost-wage calculation on day one to tax credit guidance at filing time.

Six Financial Tools in One Suite

  • Lost-Wage Calculator - hourly rate x hours/week x recovery weeks, returns estimated loss, NLDAC coverage estimate, and net out-of-pocket total
  • NLDAC Wizard - multi-step eligibility flow for National Living Donor Assistance Center (up to $6,000 reimbursement). Guides donor through every requirement
  • Expense Log - categorized CRUD (travel, lodging, childcare, medical, lost wages), receipt photo upload to encrypted AWS S3, running totals with reimbursement tracking
  • 27-State Tax Credit Guide - state-by-state reference for donor tax credits, updated annually, with direct links to state revenue department forms
  • FMLA Employer Letter Generator - printable, legally accurate letter template citing 29 CFR 825 with donor-specific fields pre-filled, PDF download
  • Insurance Issue Tracker - log coverage disputes, escalate to transplant coordinator with one click, track resolution status

Section 7 - Module 3

MentorMatch: Peer Support Network

A 2014 randomized controlled trial (Rodrigue et al., AJKD) found that peer education from a prior living donor increased donation intent by 23% among ambivalent candidates. MentorMatch operationalizes this evidence at scale - connecting willing donors with verified prior donors who match their specific situation and language.

Core Features

  • Verified mentor network of prior living kidney donors (coordinator-verified badge)
  • Multi-axis matching: donation type (laparoscopic, open, directed, non-directed, paired exchange), language, and specialty area
  • Specialty areas: financial concerns, parenting logistics, anxiety management, recovery, employer relations, spouse/partner dynamics
  • AI-assisted initial match ranking (GPT-4o) followed by coordinator-facilitated confirmation
  • HIPAA-compliant in-app messaging thread with app-layer encryption before database storage
  • Become-a-Mentor application flow for prior donors to give back to the community
  • Message read receipts and notification system

FHIR Resources

Patient (de-identified) RelatedPerson Communication
Evidence Base

Rodrigue JR et al. "Increasing the Pool of Potential Kidney Donors: How to Educate Patients and Family Members." AJKD 2014. RCT: N=243 ambivalent candidates. Peer education group: 23% higher donation intent. 18% lower anxiety scores. Effect persisted at 6-month follow-up.

Privacy Architecture

Messages are encrypted at the application layer using AES-256 before storage in the phi_data schema. Mentor full names are never shown to candidates until coordinator confirms the match. De-identified FHIR Communication resources are written to HAPI for outcomes analytics only.

LifeAfter: Post-Donation Continuity

OPTN data shows that only 59% of living donors receive nephrologist follow-up at two years. The mandatory OPTN Policy 18 reporting window ends at two years, but kidney function can decline for decades. LifeAfter extends structured follow-up to a lifetime horizon with automated OPTN/HRSA reporting, mental health screening, and a tool that finally answers the question "who does what" for both the donor and their PCP.

Core Features

  • Structured check-in form at each milestone: BP (systolic/diastolic), weight (kg), mood score (1-10), energy score (1-10), free-text notes (AES-256 encrypted)
  • Recharts trend visualizations for BP, weight, mood, and energy over all milestones
  • PHQ-2 validated 2-question depression screener at every check-in. Scores 3 or higher trigger automatic coordinator escalation alert
  • PCP Clarity Tool - disambiguates nephrologist vs. PCP responsibilities with a printable responsibility matrix. Addresses the "my PCP had no idea what to do" problem directly
  • Parent-donor specific pathway with tailored emotional support prompts
  • FHIR Bulk Export for automated OPTN Policy 18 outcomes reporting to HRSA (de-identified NDJSON)
